The Frogfish

the walking fish

The Frogfish (Antennarius Commerson) is a bottom dweller fish related to the angler fish that camouflages by covering itself in spines and extra appendages. They are considered bottom dwellers because of their sit and wait nature of hunting by disguising among colorful environments with their very wide range of colors and by using a lure shaped like their prey's food to lure in the prey in a similiar manner to their deep sea cousin the angler fish. Frogfishes are able to open their mouth and engulf their prey in 6 milliseconds when hunting

Their range of colors include

  • white
  • yellow
  • tan or brown
  • orange
  • pink
  • red
  • green
  • black

Vampire squid

vampire squid from hell

The vampire squid (Vampyroteuthis pseudoinfernalis) is a sea creature that lives in low oxygen zones, despite it's name it is not related to squids or octupus. The vampire squid 'hunts' by sticking out filaments (finer tentacles covered in hairs) to catch marine snow to eat.The vmapire squid disguises from predators by exposing it's underweb over its head (think like lifting up your shirt over your head) or eject a cloud of luminating mucus

Other relevant info

  • Vampire squids are the only remaining member of their family vampyroteuthis
  • Marine snow is made of dead animals, rotting material, and waste
  • The vampire squid has bioluminescent organs at the tip of it's webbed tentacles to produce a blue light.
